## 6. Cost-Plus Pricing Validation (Target Margins)

**Required Margins:**
- Retail (B2C): ≥ 60% → Price ≥ Cost × 2.5
- B2B (Wholesale/Favors): ≥ 50% → Wholesale Price ≥ Cost × 2.0

**Pricing Formula:**  
`Final Price = max(Cost × 2.5, Market Median × 0.9)`

This ensures:
- We never undercut cost-plus minimum
- We stay competitive (10% below market median)
- B2B can discount to ~Cost × 2.0 while maintaining 50% margin
