--- f5. CART TOTAL IS CORRECT ----------------------------------------

PASS. The cart total is the correct sum of (price × quantity) for all
items in the cart.

Tested with 1 item:
  Cart: [{"id":"p3","qty":1}]
  Product p3 (وردة بلدي / Baladi Rose Candle):
    - price field in wanas_data.products: 78 (EGP)
    - qty in cart: 1
    - expected line total: 78 × 1 = 78 جنيه

  cartTotal() function (verified by calling it directly):
    function cartTotal(){
      return CART.reduce((s,i)=>{
        const p=DATA.products.find(x=>x.id===i.id);
        return s+(p?p.price*i.qty:0);
      },0);
    }
    → returned 78 (number)

  Displayed total (#cartTotal innerText): "78 جنيه"
  Expected total:                          "78 جنيه"
  Match: ✓ correct

The total is calculated by multiplying each product's price (from
wanas_data.products[].price) by its cart quantity, summed across all
cart items, and displayed with the "جنيه" suffix. The calculation
matches the displayed value exactly.
